How this works

These stories are original fiction inspired by Konkan village tales, and each card says where it comes from. When you ask a ghost a question, the question and that one story are sent to an open-weight model (Llama or gpt-oss, served by Groq). The model is told to answer only from the story. If the story doesn’t say it, the ghost says so. This site does not save your questions.
